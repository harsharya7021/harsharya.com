Long bio · 120 words

Harsh Kumar Arya is an entrepreneur from Ratlam, now based in Delhi NCR. In 2020 he founded 98 Entertainment, an influencer-marketing and talent-management agency he bootstrapped with no outside funding: ₹15 Cr+ in revenue built, eighteen people across three cities, 57 brand clients, 12 athletes and 500+ creators, including cricketer Ishant Sharma, signed exclusively when Harsh was 21. He wound the agency down in mid-2023, while it was still growing, and graduated from the Indian School of Business with an MBA in 2025. A computer-science engineer and former Georgia Tech research scholar, he now runs Ninety-Eight 2.0, a creative-and-strategy practice for founders, helps run his family's school in the NCR, and co-writes GenZ Economics with Prof. Prasanna Tantri.
